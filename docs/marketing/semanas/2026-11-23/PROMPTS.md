# Semana del 23: prompts de las 3 imágenes nuevas (Figma, solo con el sí de Ezra)

Estilo «Lo que llega» (`.claude/skills/estilo-sndwch`): luz de día, fiel a la receta y a la porción, sin caras, sin
logos inventados (el logo oficial se monta después), sin texto en la imagen. Formato 4:5 vertical, la imagen ocupa la
mitad de abajo de la pieza.

1. **La oficina (mar 24).** Overhead photo of an office meeting table at lunchtime, natural daylight from a window. Five
   15 cm sub sandwiches, each on its own opened sheet of plain brown kraft paper, five different hands (different
   sleeves, a watch, a ring) each picking one up. Glasses of water, a notebook and a pen at the edge. One sandwich has a
   small blank yellow sticky note on its paper. No faces, no logos, no text, no screens. Realistic food photography.
2. **El 30CM (jue 26).** Eye-level side view of a 30 cm Philly cheesesteak on a soft white sub roll (sliced thin beef,
   sautéed onion, green bell pepper, melted cheddar, nothing else), just cut in half on a long wooden board. Two hands,
   one from each side, each lifting one half. Honest real portion, filling not overflowing. Daylight, light neutral
   table. No faces, no logos, no text.
3. **Invita (sáb 28).** On a light table, two items side by side in daylight: on the left a plain 500 ml plastic bottle
   of a cold drink with no label; on the right a 15 cm sub sandwich wrapped in plain brown kraft paper. Each has a small
   blank paper tag hanging from a thin string. Simple, centered, soft shadow. No logos, no text, no people.
